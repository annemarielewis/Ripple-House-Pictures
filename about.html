About Ripple House

I am a writer, director, and producer dedicated to creating entertaining, socially impactful narratives that transform underrepresented experiences into emotionally resonant films that foster empathy, connection, and meaningful dialogue. The goal of my production LLC is to help ensure that disabled filmmakers, like myself, and storytellers from low-income backgrounds have access to the support and opportunities needed to bring their stories to life.

You can read more about me and my long-term goals at this link.

My passion for filmmaking and storytelling is driven by its empathetic power to transcend and transform perceptions. Even if only for the duration of a book or a movie, a well-written story has the potential to somewhat transcend divisions by reminding us of our shared humanity. For a moment, we see the world as the main character.

I have experience writing and directing across diverse mediums, including live action, animation, and claymation. I have directed and produced a feature film currently in post-production, written four feature-length screenplays (one currently in post-production and one in pre-production), and created a ten-episode limited series. See my portfolio for work samples.

Full projects and additional work are available for review upon execution of a signed NDA.

My career in storytelling began at American Rivers, one of the nation’s leading water conservation NGOs, with offices across the country and a long history of advocacy for landmark protections such as the Clean Water Act. I independently approached the organization with an idea: use human-centered narrative storytelling to make important legal, scientific, and often complex environmental information accessible and meaningful to the general public. Rather than leading with policy or technical language, I wanted to tell stories about people and their relationships with rivers and water—and allow those stories to become the vehicle for understanding the larger issues. This was motivated by my advocacy in Western water conservation at the time and by seeing, again and again, how difficult these concepts could be for the average person to sink their teeth into.

From that idea, I conceived and pitched We Are Rivers, an educational narrative podcast. When the organization was initially unsure of the concept, I wrote, hosted, produced, and edited the first four episodes for free as a proof of concept. I was subsequently hired to continue writing, hosting, and audio-editing the series. We Are Rivers grew to more than 100,000 listens within months through word of mouth alone, earned a 4.9-star rating on Apple Podcasts, and became one of the organization’s most successful public-outreach, lead-generation, and fundraising initiatives—demonstrating the power of narrative strongly enough to inspire a broader storytelling arm within the NGO.

I also worked at Coverfly, the world’s largest screenwriting platform, where I helped create The Script Library, which became Coverfly’s most successful lead-generation tool to date. My work in the screenwriting industry also included writing educational content for WeScreenplay and helping emerging writers strengthen their craft.

I have since developed my own career as a screenwriter and filmmaker. I have written four feature screenplays and two episodic series, expanding from writing into the practical work of bringing stories all the way from the page to the screen. I wrote, produced, and directed my first feature, All the Beautiful Things, an ambitious independent production whose screenplay earned recognition in Deadline Hollywood.

Through every iteration of my career, the throughline has remained the same: using story not simply to entertain, but to translate complicated and deeply human ideas into something accessible, emotionally resonant, and difficult to ignore.
